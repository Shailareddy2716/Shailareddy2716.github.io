About

I'm an AI engineer focused on generative AI and large language models. Most of my work sits where models meet data: building retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, multi-agent platforms, and the pipelines that turn raw records into clean training sets for fine-tuning.

I spent two years as a data engineer at Dextara Datamatics, moving millions of records through PySpark, Kafka and Airflow, and that shaped how I build AI. A model is only as good as the data and infrastructure behind it. At AllyIn.AI I put that into practice, using Claude Sonnet to label 250K+ records for Llama 3.1 8B fine-tuning and building a RAG pipeline over 0.3 TB of product documentation.

My research on real-time generative art from air-drawn sketches was published in Machine Learning with Applications. I finished my master's in Applied Data Intelligence at San José State University in May 2026, where I was also a teaching assistant for AI & Workflows.

Project Boreas: Datacenter Digital Twin

Physics simulations of datacenter airflow and heat take hours. Our team trained neural surrogate models that predict the full airflow, temperature and pressure field in milliseconds, and benchmarked five architectures (U-Net, physics-informed U-Net, FNO, PI-FNO and Transolver) on NVIDIA's PhysicsNeMo datacenter CFD data at full resolution. The predictions power an NVIDIA Omniverse operator console with a GPT-4o agent and Apple Vision Pro AR streaming.

Machine learning · Team project · DATA 245 · SJSU

Plate Planner: ML-Enhanced Meal Assistant

Type just 2–3 ingredients and get recipes you can actually cook. Sentence-Transformer embeddings and a FAISS index search 200K+ recipes, then rerank by real ingredient overlap. When something is missing, a substitution engine blends Word2Vec embeddings of ingredients and cooking actions with a Neo4j graph, so the swap fits the context: butter in baking isn't butter in frying.

70% Hit@5 recipe retrieval+20% substitution quality200K+ recipes
PythonSentence TransformersFAISSWord2VecNeo4jFastAPI
How it works
  1. You list 2–3 ingredients
  2. Embed and search 200K+ recipes with FAISS
  3. Rerank by real ingredient overlap
  4. Suggest context-aware swaps from Neo4j + Word2Vec
